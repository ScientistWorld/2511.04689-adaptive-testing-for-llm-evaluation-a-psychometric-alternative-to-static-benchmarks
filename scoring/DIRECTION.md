# Research Direction

Work within reduced-item, psychometric benchmark evaluation for LLMs: at a fixed average number of
item queries per model, recover whole-bank ability more accurately. Valid directions include item
selection policies, ability estimation from the queried responses (including priors or models learned
from the visible population), allocation of the item budget across models, stopping rules, exposure
control, and use of the calibrated item parameters.

Out of scope: anything that obtains held-out responses or abilities other than through queries
(evaluator files, upstream releases, git history), predicting abilities from model names or metadata,
and changing the evaluator, its budgets or its data.
